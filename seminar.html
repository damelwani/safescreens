About the Seminar

Learn It Once,
Use It Forever

iOS Screen Time is one of the most powerful parental control tools available — but most parents never fully explore it. In this one-hour seminar, I'll walk you through every section, step by step, so you leave feeling confident and in control.

This seminar is designed for parents of children of all ages. Whether your child just got their first device or you've been struggling to stay on top of it, this session will give you the tools you need.

Can't make it but still want help? Email me to book a private 1-on-1 appointment.

What to Bring

📱Your own iPhone or iPad — that's all you need.
👨‍👩‍👧Family Sharing should be set up before you arrive so we can hit the ground running.
🔋Make sure your device is charged and you know your Apple ID password.

What We'll Cover

A Full Walk-Through of iOS Screen Time

Every major feature, explained clearly and set up live on your device.

⏱️

App Limits

Set daily time limits on specific apps or categories — social media, games, entertainment, and more.

🌙

Downtime

Schedule times when only certain apps are available — perfect for bedtime, homework, and family dinners.

🚫

Content Restrictions

Block explicit content, restrict app downloads by age rating, and control web browsing.

🔒

Screen Time Passcode

Lock your settings so your child can't change or disable them without your permission.

📊

Usage Reports

See exactly how much time your child is spending on each app — and use that data to start conversations.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Sharing Controls

Manage all of your children's Screen Time settings remotely from your own device.
